Individual insolvency statistics - July 2026 Body The July 2026 individual insolvency stats opens new window for England and Wales have been published by The Insolvency Service, together with related figures for Scotland and Northern Ireland. Individual insolvency statistics for England and Wales – July 2026 In July 2026, 11,926 individuals entered insolvency in England & Wales. This was similar to in June 2026 but 14% higher than in July 2025.

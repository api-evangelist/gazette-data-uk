The King’s Award for Voluntary Service 2026 Body This year's winners of the King’s Award for Voluntary Service will be announced in a special Gazette supplement on Saturday 14 November 2026. What is The King’s Award for Voluntary Service? The King's Award for Voluntary Service (previously Queen's Award for Voluntary Service) is the highest award given to UK volunteer groups, being equivalent to the M.B.E .
